How to practise
  1. Hold the phone flat, screen up, in your cup hand, the way you'd hold a cup by its base.
  2. Hold an empty pitcher in your other hand and mime the pour over the screen. No real liquid.
  3. Turn on Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Centre, or the screen flips sideways when you tilt.
  4. Follow the voice cues. The ring turns green when your cup tilt is on target.
  5. After each pour you get a tilt chart, a score per phase and one thing to fix.
